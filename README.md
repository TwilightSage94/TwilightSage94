### The lab behind the applications

- **Rover** is the service-desk operator. Its authenticated technician workspace is running inside Watchtower. The newer requester guidance and signed intake workflow have passed local checks; live transport, identity, status, and recovery qualification remain open.
- **Watchtower** is the enterprise lab where I test identity, access, tickets, and human handoffs. Its illustrated issues follow the build as it changes, with dated checks beside the story.
- **Tower** is the Linux compute host for local models, applications, and storage. J-Bot and Twilight have their own workspaces and responsibilities there. Access between services needs its own checks.

## How I work

1. Understand the person, the environment, and the problem.
2. Decide what the system should access, remember, and change.
3. Connect the interface, tools, data, and infrastructure.
4. Try the real workflow, keep the results, and fix what fails.

AI helps me build and coordinate the work. I stay responsible for the decisions around access, publishing, money, and consequential changes. The useful question is whether the test measures what people actually need the system to do.

I'm interested in work that brings this experience together: endpoint deployments, identity, automation, and AI tools that people can use in their everyday work.
